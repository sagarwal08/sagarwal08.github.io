Privacy policy — delta-capture

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who uses it

delta-capture is a personal tool used only by its owner. It has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

  • It uses the Google Drive scope drive.file: it can only see and manage files and folders that it created itself in the owner's Drive.
  • It cannot read any other file in the Drive, and it does not access email, contacts, or any other Google data.

What it stores

Only publicly available market data, uploaded to the owner's own Google Drive. It collects no personal information about anyone.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. Nothing is used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
